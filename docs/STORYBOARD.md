# STORYBOARD — "Whisper Of Hope" · a Clawd MV

Song: 153.75 s. BPM 120, first downbeat 0.25 s → bar lines at 0.25 + 2k (all shot cuts sit on bar lines).
Music map: silent intro 0–26 · verse 26–50 · build 50–70 · hush 70–74.25 · chorus risers 74.25/86.25/98.25 ·
peak 110–133 · outro decay 133–153.75.

Logline: Clawd is alone in the cold rain, but a tiny guttering light — a whisper of hope — drifts to it; Clawd
shields it through the storm and breathes it back to life, and when the little light bursts into the dawn, Clawd
grows a spark of its own and sends it toward the one corner of the world still grey.

World: a rainy fir meadow at night. Palette arc (the whole film is this one arc): indigo rain-night → teal thaw →
ochre/rose dawn. Clawd's body colour warms with the world. Motif: the **ripple** — puddle rings, the flare ring,
the sunburst, the spark's trail. Travel is always screen-right until the last shot sends the spark left (back,
for someone else).

Characters: Clawd (the kit's) · the **ember** — a small round light, faint, flickering like a whisper; wakes with
two dot eyes · three **moss puffs** — round grey-green critters with a sprout on top, desynced bounce.

Arc (emotion keys): sad/sleepy → curious → scared/determined (shielding) → hopeful (the whisper) →
joy/laugh (revival) → proud/wave (friends) → determined reach (climb) → starstruck (the burst) →
relieved/hopeful → love (passing it on).
